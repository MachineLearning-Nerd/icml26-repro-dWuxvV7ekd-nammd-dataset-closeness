# Method

Closed-form Gaussian kernel expectations recompute the four norms, two cross
terms, MMD², NAMMD, norm order, and the complete integer C1/C2 window. Every
window value is checked against the 1/65 power-advantage bound and the first
implication's probability bound. An independent adaptive quadrature recomputes
the kernel expectations. Reversing the norm ordering is the negative control.
