# Source audit

The judge's claim is v1 Theorem 5, renumbered Theorem 7 in v3. The exact bound
is `(2*z_(1-alpha)+sqrt(9*log(2/v)))² / gap²`, with
`gap=NAMMD(P,Q;k)-epsilon>0`. The paper calls inverse-square the main quantity
dictating sample complexity. It does not claim that a three-point fixed-sample
power curve proves the exponent or that the sufficient bound is necessary.
